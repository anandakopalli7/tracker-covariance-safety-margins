## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor): From the methodology seat, this dimension asks whether the empirical design is able to answer the questions the manuscript poses for itself. I will judge whether the evaluation isolates the comparison it claims to make; whether the units of analysis and their dependence structure (where present: repeated runs, shared random seeds, shared scenarios or initial conditions) are honoured by the inferential procedure; whether every uncertainty statement comes from a procedure that is valid for the statistic actually reported, including clustered and non-smooth statistics such as rates, quantiles, extremes or thresholded outcomes; whether the selection of reported contrasts and multiplicity are controlled; and whether code, configurations, seeds and per-run data are documented well enough for an independent team to regenerate the reported numbers. It is mandatory and my seat owns it.

D2 (domain_accuracy): This dimension concerns whether the manuscript's statements about its field and about earlier results are correct and fairly represented. It belongs to the domain seat. From a methodology standpoint it matters only where a design or analysis choice is justified by appeal to a prior result or an accepted standard, since a misstated precedent would remove that justification. I will not score it.

D3 (argumentative_coherence): This dimension asks whether the central thesis is internally consistent and whether each claim is carried by the evidence actually presented, with no load-bearing assumption left unsupported, including assumptions about how gains, burdens and exclusions fall across the affected parties. The devil's-advocate seat owns it, but it is eligible for mine; my lens is the inferential chain from analysis to conclusion: whether the strength of each conclusion matches the strength of its statistical support, whether results from a restricted or simulated population are generalised beyond it without argument, whether an inconclusive comparison is read as equivalence, and whether the claimed explanation is the only one the design permits.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance): A high-priority dimension owned by the perspective seat. It asks whether readers from neighbouring disciplines can follow the framing, terminology and consequences, and whether claims that cross field boundaries are earned. Methodologically I note only that claims about evaluation practice made to another field need the same evidential footing as claims made within the home field. I will not score it.

D5 (writing_and_structure): A normal-priority dimension owned by the editor-in-chief seat, covering how the manuscript is laid out, how clearly it explains itself, how well its tables and figures work, and whether it follows the outlet's house style. Its methodological relevance is limited to whether tables and figures show sample sizes and uncertainty legibly. I will not score it.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution): A mandatory dimension owned by the editor-in-chief seat. It asks whether the chosen outlet is the right home for the work and whether what it adds is novel and important to that outlet's readers. A contribution claim resting on an unsound comparison would be weakened, but judging fit and significance is outside my remit. I will not score it.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor
dimension_id: D1
what_to_look_for: A design that isolates the stated comparison at a declared and verified operating point; a named independent unit of analysis with a replication count and a justified stopping rule; uncertainty intervals from a procedure valid for the statistic and its dependence (cluster or block resampling over the true independent units, calibrated or double bootstrap or subsampling for non-smooth or boundary statistics, exact or score intervals for rare-event proportions); Monte Carlo error reported for simulation-derived quantities; pre-specified primary metrics and contrasts with multiplicity control; sensitivity analyses over arbitrary tuning and configuration choices; and released code, configurations, seeds and per-run outputs sufficient for regeneration.
what_triggers_block: A correctable but conclusion-relevant inferential defect: intervals or tests that treat dependent runs as independent, a resampling scheme applied to a non-smooth or boundary statistic without justification or calibration, uncontrolled multiplicity across the reported contrasts, an operating point that is claimed to be matched across compared arms but is not verified, or missing information needed to reproduce a headline number, where the defect could plausibly change a headline conclusion and can be repaired by reanalysis or additional runs.
what_triggers_warn: Reporting or robustness gaps that do not threaten a headline conclusion: replication counts or seeds stated but not justified, absent coverage diagnostics or Monte Carlo error for secondary quantities, sensitivity analyses confined to a narrow range of settings, or reproducibility materials described but incomplete.
what_triggers_fatal: A design flaw that no reanalysis can repair: the comparison as constructed cannot identify the claimed effect because the compared arms differ in a confounded way the data cannot separate, the headline result rests on post hoc selection of metrics, settings or runs that cannot be undone, or reported numbers cannot be traced to any described procedure.

### D3: argumentative_coherence
dimension_id: D3
what_to_look_for: Each headline conclusion paired with the specific analysis that supports it at matching strength; explicit scope conditions limiting generalisation beyond the evaluated settings; non-significant or inconclusive comparisons not read as equivalence without a stated equivalence margin; rival explanations that the design leaves open acknowledged; and every load-bearing premise, including any about which parties gain, bear cost or are excluded, either supported or explicitly flagged as an assumption.
what_triggers_block: A central claim that outruns its evidence in a way revision can fix: a conclusion stated more strongly or more generally than the presented analysis licenses, absence of a detected difference read as equivalence without a margin, a rival explanation the design cannot exclude left unaddressed, or a load-bearing premise (including one about beneficiaries, cost-bearers or excluded parties) asserted without support.
what_triggers_warn: Local overstatement or loose wording that leaves the central thesis intact: a secondary claim phrased somewhat beyond its support, scope limits present but placed where readers may miss them, or a peripheral premise asserted without citation or argument.
what_triggers_fatal: The core thesis contradicts the manuscript's own reported results, or depends on a premise that the manuscript's own evidence refutes, so the central argument fails as stated and cannot be rescued by rewording or added qualification.
