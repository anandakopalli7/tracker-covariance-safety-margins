## Summary of changes

The review judged that rewording no longer moves the paper, and asked for evidence the panel could not get from the same 35 runs. v13 supplies it under a plan approved and hashed before any new value was computed, and recenters the paper on the evaluation protocol.

**New evidence.**
1. **Fresh runs, scored once.** 105 lead-braking and 105 cut-in runs from never-run cases of the same generator, with every sweep kept, scored once under fixed hypothesis sequences, a gridless estimand, an equivalence margin of 0.05 of the room and calibrated intervals.
2. **An amplitude-matched control.** An amplitude-matched ranking oracle, which orders decisions by their realized error within a ±5% band, tests whether the score can detect rank information in that band (R5); the same construction at each tracker's own spread gives every covariance a reference at its own amplitude, and at its spread the tracker of record is amplitude-limited. It is a reference, not a bound: a covariance whose shape differs from the class average's can score above it.
3. **Other trackers' covariances.** An IMM tracker (CV/CA) as tuned here, an innovation-adaptive filter, a CV filter retuned to a median NIS near 2, and a CA filter, all tuned on tuning runs only and frozen before scoring (R2, R10).
4. **A closed-loop test** in CARLA with the margin sources as arms of the planner driving the car, behind a braking fix gated by a brake test fixed in advance. The brake test passed and the main runs completed (Section V-C). The per-phase schedule's closed-loop arm is not caution-matched: the preregistered phase-scale rule bounds its caution by F0's level, and its design caution is below F0's at both levels, so its closed-loop comparisons are descriptive; it carries no hypothesis.
5. **A real-sensor replication** on nuScenes, design and held-out splits separated. The held-out validation scenes were scored once (Section V-A). The braking prediction, the acceleration margin, and the NIS inflation are not computed on the real logs: the first two use the filter's acceleration-sign history and the run's primary actor, and the third the innovations of the capture's own filter, none of which the real-log tracker provides. This departure from the plan was recorded before any real-log value (Supplement S3); no real-log hypothesis uses these sources. A second departure concerns the clearance: under the planned clearance the design split showed no room, because replayed traffic behind the ego strikes the planner's hard-brake fallback. After that result, and before any held-out scene was read, contacts with actors behind the ego were no longer counted; the planned outcome is printed beside the changed one (Section III-C; Supplement S10).
6. **Conditions measured, not assumed:** nonbraking leads (three variants), update rate and latency (5 and 10 Hz from kept sweeps), filter tuning, lead-deceleration terciles, and a delayed stop.
