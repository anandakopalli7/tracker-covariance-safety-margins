## Contract Paraphrase

D1 (methodology_rigor): From an editorial-oversight standpoint, this dimension asks whether the way the study was designed, how its data were collected and processed, how its statistics are reported, and what it offers others for reproduction would satisfy a competent methods referee in this field. It is mandatory and only the methodology seat may score it; I do not score it, but I note that a fatal finding there forces rejection and a block there forces major revision under the contract's failure conditions, so the panel outcome depends on it.

D2 (domain_accuracy): This dimension asks whether what the manuscript asserts is consistent with the current body of evidence in its domain, whether earlier work is described faithfully rather than mischaracterised, and whether specialised terms and reported findings are free of factual mistakes. It is mandatory and reserved to the domain seat; I do not score it, and as editor I would treat a domain-seat block as a reason the manuscript cannot be accepted in its present form.

D3 (argumentative_coherence): This dimension asks whether the central thesis hangs together without internal contradiction, whether the evidence offered actually carries the claims made, whether any reasoning error undercuts the main argument, and whether any premise a core claim leans on, including assumptions about who gains, who pays, or who is excluded, is left without support. It is mandatory, open to the devil's-advocate and methodology seats, and owned by the devil's advocate; I do not score it.

D4 (cross_disciplinary_relevance): This dimension asks whether the framing, the definitions, and the stated implications can be followed by readers from neighbouring disciplines, and whether any claim that crosses a disciplinary boundary is backed rather than asserted. It is high priority and reserved to the perspective seat; I do not score it, though a block there on its own would push the outcome to major revision.

D5 (writing_and_structure): This dimension is mine to score. It asks whether the manuscript is organised so a reader can follow the line from problem to finding, whether the prose explains rather than obscures, whether figures and tables are legible and carry the information they claim to, and whether the submission respects the venue's conventions on structure, length, abstract, and referencing. It has normal priority and is not mandatory, so at worst it moves the outcome toward minor revision through the any-dimension warn condition; it cannot by itself force rejection.

D6 (venue_fit_and_contribution): This dimension is mine to score and is mandatory. It asks whether the work belongs in the configured journal at all and whether it gives that journal's readers something new and consequential rather than a restatement of what they already know. Because it is mandatory, a fatal finding here leads to rejection, a block leads to major revision, and a warn counts toward the condition under which two or more mandatory warns force major revision.

## Scoring Plan

### D5: writing_and_structure
dimension_id: D5
what_to_look_for: A clear problem-to-finding arc (motivation, related work, method, experimental setup, results, discussion, limitations, conclusion) where each section has one job; an abstract that states problem, approach, headline quantitative result and implication within the venue's abstract word limit; contributions stated explicitly near the start and mapped to sections; acronyms, notation and metrics defined at first use and used consistently; figures and tables legible at print size with labelled axes and units, self-contained captions, uncertainty shown wherever results are compared, and each one cited and interpreted in the text; total length proportionate to the contribution and within the venue's recommended length; front matter, reference style and formatting in venue style with no leftover drafting artifacts.
what_triggers_block: Exposition so deficient that an adjacent transportation-systems reader cannot reconstruct what was done or what was found without guessing, for example headline results that appear only in unreadable, mislabelled or text-contradicting figures or tables, core metrics or notation never defined, an ordering that separates methods from the results they produce so claims cannot be traced, or a length roughly 30 percent or more beyond the venue's recommendation driven by redundancy, or an abstract far over the word limit, so that the manuscript is not ready to be sent out for review.
what_triggers_warn: The manuscript is followable but needs editing before acceptance, for example an abstract modestly over the limit or missing the headline number, a vague contribution list not tied to sections, scattered undefined acronyms or drifting terminology, figures with small fonts, missing units or non-self-contained captions, redundant passages or appendix-grade detail in the main text pushing length somewhat above the venue's recommendation, or minor reference-style, formatting or typographical problems.

### D6: venue_fit_and_contribution
dimension_id: D6
what_to_look_for: Whether the research question and its answer matter to the configured intelligent-transportation journal's readers (researchers and practitioners in automated driving and driver assistance); a contribution statement that is explicit and distinguishable from the closest prior work the authors themselves position against; an argument for significance showing that the result would change what a researcher, tester or developer in this area does or believes, not merely a report of one configuration; claims scoped in proportion to the evidence base described; and whether the work is primarily a transportation-systems contribution rather than one that would sit more naturally in a general robotics, control or machine-learning outlet.
what_triggers_block: The work is within scope but its contribution is not yet established at the level this journal expects, for example the novelty claim is asserted but not differentiated from the closest prior work, the significance for the readership is not argued, or the transportation-systems framing is so thin that substantial repositioning, added comparison against prior approaches, or narrowed claims are needed, all of which appear achievable within one major revision.
what_triggers_warn: The work is in scope and makes a genuine but incremental or under-argued contribution, for example the contribution list overstates or blurs what is new, implications for testing practice or follow-on research are left implicit, or positioning against prior work is incomplete, all of which can be fixed by rewriting the framing, contribution statement and discussion without new experiments.
what_triggers_fatal: The manuscript lies outside the configured journal's scope because it poses no substantive intelligent-transportation or automated-driving question its readers would recognise, or it offers no identifiable original contribution because it reproduces an established result, substantially duplicates prior published work, or would not change any reader's understanding or practice, so that no revision of this submission could make it publishable in this venue.
