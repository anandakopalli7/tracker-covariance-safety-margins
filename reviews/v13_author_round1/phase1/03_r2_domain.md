## 4. The eight answers

### 1. Labels and sizes
Mostly right. "Confirmed" appears only for the original sequence (Table 2: room, detection, N's equivalence, the IMM's class, the schedule's transfer, the braking prediction). Amendment results carry "(A)", "replicated" or "failed"; Holm sits beside the labels in Table 6 and V-E for the six second-amendment primaries and, separately, the two third-amendment controls (adjusted p 0.99 and 0.18), and changes none. Sizes are given as small ("detectable, but small by that margin"). Remaining issues: the coast-count result is stated as "as much" when the coast count did slightly better (R2-m2); the abstract's 4.6% for the adaptive filter is its descriptive share, while its test is the twin difference (0.0498, then 0.0508), which is acceptable only because Table 3 labels it; the closed-loop row "(A), H 0.0003" in Table 6 lacks the flag that it was not matched in closed loop (R2-o5).

### 2. Overreach
- **Dropout gain's source.** Largely corrected: the abstract, I, V-E and VII all say a count of missed updates reproduces it. Left: "as much", a dropout-blind comparator, and a control run at one rate only (R2-m2); the 10 Hz juxtaposition, whose class average is the 2 Hz one (R2-m3). What the failed control leaves: a coast-aware margin recovers about 3% of the lead-braking room at 16% masked updates; the fixed-noise covariance supplies such a margin automatically, which is a convenience, not information about the error; ranking within dropouts is not shown; and given [27], the equivalence was predictable beforehand.
- **Closed loop.** Stated as what it is: "fewer contacts ... were not shown at equal time lost" (abstract), "The fewer contacts thus cannot be told from a wider margin" (V-E). The nominal closed loop's wording "no contact difference above 0.10 was resolved" understates the equivalence form and its wide margin (R2-m9).
- **nuScenes mechanism check.** The training-split choice and the inverse all-pairs relation are disclosed; the level reading is given. Missing: which classes form the pairs (R2-m5).
- **Real-log decision test.** Correct: "found no room ... so nothing was tested there".
- **Two-noise IMM.** The position-velocity limit is stated in V-E and VI, but not that the margin omits process noise over the look-ahead or that the maneuver mode is a CA filter whose acceleration is dropped, and the abstract and VII state its share without the qualifier (R2-M2).
- **Scope of the null.** The prediction model is named in the abstract and VII ("with a constant-velocity prediction"); the planner class is named only in I (R2-m8).
