### 2. Overreach

- **Closed loop.** At the right size: "passed (amendment)" for fewer contacts, then "cannot be told from a wider margin", then "failed" at equal time lost. The failed test is low-powered (interval −0.12 to 0.02 on 135 cases), and the text says "not shown", not "absent". No figure (O-1); population mixing not named as a limit (O-2).

- One remaining overreach: "recovered nothing" in Section VII (Rm-4).
