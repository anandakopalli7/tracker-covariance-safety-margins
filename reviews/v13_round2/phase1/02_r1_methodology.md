## The five answers

**1. True size and right label?** Mostly yes. Every checked number is reproduced by both programs. "Confirmed" is kept for the original plan; amendment tests carry "passed (amendment)"; the replications carry "replicated under rules fixed before the data", which the locks support for scoring (finding 8 for the one overstatement). Holm across the six round-2 primaries is reported and correct. Sizes are stated small ("a little", "a small 2.8%"). The exceptions are finding 7 (a negative τ reported as "not") and finding 12 (abstract shares without intervals).

**2. Does any sentence claim more than its test allows?**
- *Dropout gain's source:* the body is honest (level effect, fixed-increment control untested). The abstract's and heading's "tracks the error" claims more (finding 3), and one stratum was reported selectively (finding 4).
- *Caution-matched closed loop:* yes, it over-claims in the abstract and conclusion. The match is open-loop; closed-loop caution differs; the reference is at the grid bottom and far off its calibration (findings 1, 2).
- *nuScenes mechanism check:* the train-informed primary is disclosed in the main text. The reading "ranks the realised error around real missed detections" is stronger than a mostly missed-against-steady τ of 0.11 allows (finding 6).
- *Real-log decision test:* reported correctly as no measurable room, nothing tested, outside the Holm family.

**3. The previous round's limits.**

| Limit | Status | What remains |

| Unfair IMM arm | Addressed | A two-noise IMM that reaches its NIS window, tested on new runs. It was tuned on braking for a braking test, and its gain is a phase-level effect (finding 13). |
| Closed loop not caution-matched | Partly addressed | Matched in open loop under dropouts; not in closed loop; one level at the grid bottom; no second class-average scale (findings 1, 2). |

**4. Readability.** The abstract gives the bottom line first, but its first sentence carries a parenthesis and a universal "only", and at 250 words it packs eleven numbers. Coined terms are defined once in Table I; "caution-matched" is used in the abstract before the open-loop qualifier appears in V-E. Each claim's evidence can be found: V-E points to S14/S15 and Tables S37–S44 map one to one. Section V-E (774 words, no main-text table) is the hardest part to audit; a small main-text table of the second amendment's six primaries with their Holm values would help more than any sentence.

**5. Recommendation.** **Major revision** (light: text changes plus one offline control and, if possible, one extra closed-loop arm). **Score 6.5 / 10; 3.5 stars of 5.** Most likely editorial outcome at IEEE OJ-ITS: major revision, with reviewers asking for the closed-loop caution point and the dropout-flag control. **The single change that would raise this seat's score most:** print the class average with a fixed margin increment during dropouts (open loop, offline) beside the covariance's 3.0%, and reword the closed-loop claim to "matched by open-loop caution" with the closed-loop time and rest-gap differences stated; with both, this seat would move to minor revision, about 7.5.
