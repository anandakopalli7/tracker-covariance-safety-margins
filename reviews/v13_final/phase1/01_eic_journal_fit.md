## Answers

**1. Labels and sizes.** Largely right, and better than a typical paper. "Confirmed" appears only for the original plan (room, detection in a ±5% band, tracker equivalence, IMM class, per-phase schedule, braking prediction). Amendment tests read "passed (amendment)", "failed", or "replicated" (Table 6, V-E). Holm for the six second-amendment primaries is in Table 6 (0.0079, 0.0003) and changes none; the third amendment's two (0.18 and 0.99, S38) are said only to "fail regardless" (J13). The closed-loop equivalence is "the plan's class", not "confirmed", which is correct. Sizes: the dropout share (0.0283, 0.0298) and the two-noise IMM (0.0220) are given as small and inside the margin, with "no equivalence test was registered". Remaining slips: J4 (pedestrian "other reasons" stated flat), J11 (closed-loop equivalence without level), J12 (Table 3 classes missing).

**2. Overreach.** Dropout gain's source: handled; the text says a coast-count margin recovered slightly more and the gain is a "level effect that needs no covariance". The abstract's "gains a little where it grows" is acceptable next to its own "but". Closed loop: handled ("might come from a wider margin"; equal time lost "not shown"); the class average missed its calibration target (17% on tuning, 33% on test), which weakens the 33% comparator and is disclosed. nuScenes mechanism: the training-split choice of population is disclosed in V-E but not in the abstract; the text itself says "this too is partly a level effect"; acceptable. Real-log decision test: "no real-log decision test was possible" is accurate. Two-noise IMM: "lower bound" is an inference (J10). Also J9 (heading of VI) and J4.

**3. Earlier limits.**
- (a) Closed loop matched only in open-loop caution: **partly addressed**. The equal-time-lost comparison was run and failed; text and abstract say so. Remains: one level, 135 cases, class average mis-calibrated, lead braking only.
- (b) Dropout gain vs coasting margin: **addressed**; the control was run and reported. Remains: no covariance-specific gain is shown.
- (c) Abstract and conclusion stronger than the body: **mostly addressed**; "only where it varies", "where it tracks the error" and "shows on real data" are gone. Remains: J4, J9, J11, and abstract density (J1).
- (d) Two-noise IMM interface: **partly addressed**; a two-noise IMM was added and flagged. Remains: same position-velocity interface; J10 wording.
- (e) Holm and recount scope: **addressed**; IV-E states Holm's family and the recount scope (main text and all but ‡ supplement values; shared library, arrays, draws). Remains: J13, J16.
- (f) Density, round-2 results only in the supplement: **partly addressed**; Section V-D and V-E and Tables 5 and 6 are in the main text. Remains: 9 Q6 cases and J7 (density now the problem).
- Structural, unfixable by wording: a confirmed result that could hardly fail, small positive effects, one simulator and configuration, no decision-level real-log evidence, and the process (amendments after results, local hash locks, AI authorship). All remain; the text discloses each.

**4. Readability.** Bottom line first: yes, the first abstract sentence. Coined terms: defined once in Table 1, but late (p. 4) and incompletely (J7, Q7-12). Evidence findable: mostly, each claim points to a table; V-E is the hard part, and the nine Q6 cases show where the pointer leads outside the main text.
