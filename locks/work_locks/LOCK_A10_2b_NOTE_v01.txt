- Reading of A10 7.3 ("For PS, its H4 triple at that caution level"): the H4 choice rule of A10 5.5 applied at the level l = F0's open-loop caution at g_L: among the scale triples whose design caution is at most l, the one with the lowest wrong-go; ties to the lowest caution, then the lowest index. The rule bounds the triple's caution by l; it does not target l.
- Result: a triple with zero design wrong-go exists at design caution 11.8775 %, below both levels, so the rule returns the same triple at both levels: (before braking, braking, stopped) = (125.89254117941688, 105.92537251772897, 0.44668359215096326), grid indices (165, 162, 67). F0's open-loop caution is 14.1001 % at lo (g = 4) and 22.2951 % at hi (g = 16). Every other arm is matched within 0.53 percentage points of F0.
